# Trailhead Co. Store Notes

Trail Runner 3 is the newest trail shoe. It is stable on mixed terrain, has the same last as Trail Runner 2, and works best for shoppers who want the current model. Because it just landed, the shopkeeper should not imply the shoe itself is discounted.

Trail Runner 2 is last season's shoe with the same fit as Trail Runner 3. It is a good recommendation when a shopper likes Trail Runner 3 but has a lower budget.

Ridge Lite is the budget trail shoe. It is lighter and less protective than Trail Runner 3, but it is a good fit for shorter runs and shoppers who are strict on budget.

Merino Socks pair with any long run. They are the simplest add-on when the shopper mentions distance, comfort, blisters, or anything over 20 km.

Trail Gaiters pair with muddy or scree-heavy routes. Recommend them when the shopper mentions mud, loose rock, racing, or a 50k.

Soft Flask is for self-supported runs and race days. Recommend it when the shopper mentions hydration, longer efforts, or carrying less hard gear.

Race Vest is for long trail days where pockets and bottles matter. Trail Cap is a small add-on for sun and heat.
